# Operator guide — one strategy, start to finish

*One page. No technical knowledge assumed. Follow it top to bottom and you will produce a sourced, board-ready deck.*

You are working inside a single **engagement** (one client, one strategy question). Open it and you'll see nine tabs across the top. Work them left to right — each builds on the one before, and the system won't let you skip the evidence.

The golden rule of this tool: **nothing exists without a source.** Every downstream artifact — the SWOT, the options, the deck — traces back to facts you entered, with dates and where they came from. That chain is the product.

---

### 1. Signals — the facts (tab: **Signals**)
A signal is one sourced fact. Click **Add signal** and fill in:
- **The excerpt** — what was said or found, in a sentence or two.
- **Source** — either a web link (URL) *or* an interview reference ("CFO, Q3 review call"). An interview is a perfectly valid source.
- **Published date** and **retrieval date** — when it was said/written, and when you captured it.
- **Dimension** — which lens it informs (political, economic, market, competitor, internal, customer…).
- **Credibility** — your confidence in the source.

You cannot save a signal without a source and a date. That is deliberate.

### 2. Insights — the "so what" (tab: **Insights**)
An insight is your interpretation. Click **Add insight**, write the point, set a confidence level, and **link at least one signal** that backs it. No linked signal, no insight — the button won't save it.

### 3. Capabilities — where you stand (tab: **Capabilities**)
The client's capabilities are already listed. For each, set **current maturity** and **required maturity** (1–5) and its **criticality**. The **heatmap** turns red where the gap is widest. This is the same heatmap that appears in the deck — no surprises later.

### 4. SWOT — let the system draft it (tab: **SWOT**)
Click **Derive SWOT**. The AI reads your capability gaps and your dimension-tagged signals and drafts Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats — each item carrying the exact evidence it rests on. You edit the wording. If you delete an item, the system asks **why**, so evidence is never quietly discarded.

### 5. Options — let the system generate the field (tab: **Options**)
Click **Generate options**. The AI produces at least five genuinely different growth options, each with its bet, prerequisites, the strongest argument *against* it, and its evidence. **It does not rank them and does not recommend one** — on purpose. That is the machine's job done.

### 6. Choice — your decision, and only yours (tab: **Choice**)
This is the one thing the machine cannot do. Pick the option you're backing, record the **alternatives you considered**, your **rationale**, **who decided**, and a **revisit trigger** (what would make you reopen this). This writes the decision log. The deck's final slide is built from exactly what you enter here.

### 7. Coherence — the safety check (tab: **Coherence**)
Click **Run checks**. The system flags gaps in the logic — an insight with no sourced signal, a choice that doesn't trace to evidence, a critical capability gap nobody addressed. For each finding you can fix it, or **accept it with a note** (a strategy may knowingly carry a gap — but now it's recorded, not forgotten).

### 8. Deck — the deliverable (tab: **Deck**)
Click **Generate & download deck**. In under a minute you get a **PowerPoint file** — seven slides, editable text and native charts (it opens correctly in PowerPoint *and* Google Slides). Cover, evidence base, capability heatmap, priority gaps, SWOT, options considered, and your choice with its rationale.

### 9. Provenance — "why is this here?" (any node → **View chain**)
Anywhere you see an item, you can click through to its full upstream chain: this item ← these insights ← these sourced signals, with dates and sources. Point at anything in the deck and the tool answers where it came from. That is the whole promise of the platform, and it works on every artifact you just created.

---

**If it won't let you proceed,** that's the enforcement working, not a bug: a signal needs a source, an insight needs a signal, a choice needs a logged rationale. Supply the missing evidence and continue.
